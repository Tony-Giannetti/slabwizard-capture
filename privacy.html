SlabWizard Capture — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

SlabWizard Capture photographs stone slabs and delivers them to the SlabWizard application on your own computer. It is built so that we never receive, store, or see your data.

Where your photos go

Photos and slab details upload to a folder in your own Google Drive, using the account you sign in with on the phone. Your SlabWizard PC signs into the same account and imports them from there. Nothing is sent to SlabWizard's servers — there are none in this pipeline.

What access the app has

The app requests Google's drive.file permission, the narrowest Drive scope: it can only see and manage files it created itself (the "SlabWizard Captures" folder). It cannot read the rest of your Drive, your email, or anything else in your Google account.

What stays on the phone

Settings (your site code, device name) and any captures queued while offline are stored locally on the phone and uploaded to your Drive when there is signal. Removing the app removes them.

What we collect

Nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no accounts with us. A capture carries a device-side diagnostic log to help troubleshoot imports; it travels only inside your capture bundle, in your Drive, to your PC.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your captured slabs remain in your Drive and your SlabWizard inventory.
